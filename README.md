## What it does

Tell your AI Sales Director who you want as customers. It finds the right people, writes to each one personally, answers the replies and books the meetings, inside a complete CRM.

## How the Office works

| | | |
|:-:|---|---|
| **1** | **You set the goal** | One sentence: who you want as customers. |
| **2** | **The Director plans** | The AI Sales Director turns the goal into directions and hands the work to its Agents. |
| **3** | **Finding customers** | The right companies, and the right people in them. |
| **4** | **Writing to each one** | Every email written for its person, never a mass template. |
| **5** | **Answering replies** | Replies answered, questions about prices included. |
| **6** | **Booking meetings** | Interested people get a meeting in your calendar. |

## A complete CRM around it

Command Center · Goals & Experiments · Lead Intelligence · Pipeline and forecast · Relationships · Activities · Campaigns · Audience · Communications · Analytics · Office Brain

## You stay in control

The Office works through the tools you already use, and only as far as you allow. Anything important waits for your yes: the Director shows what it wants to send, to whom and when.
